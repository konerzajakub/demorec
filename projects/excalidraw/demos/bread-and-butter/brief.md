# bread-and-butter

Level 2 of the Excalidraw ladder. Level 1 is `first-diagram`.

## Audience

Developers who saw the level 1 video and want to know whether demorec handles a longer,
denser demo.

## Goal

The viewer sees a real diagram built step by step: a title, ingredients, recipe steps, arrows,
colors and a note, ending in a clean overview of the whole recipe.

## Story

A guest in Excalidraw draws the recipe for bread with butter and chives. Ingredients on the left,
the steps in a row to the right, arrows show which ingredient goes into which step, and the
recipe ends in the finished dish.

## Key moments

1. Blank canvas: the blank canvas with the toolbar
2. Title: the text "Bread with butter and chives" at the top of the canvas
3. Ingredients: four shapes in a column on the left labelled "bread", "butter", "chives", "salt"
4. Steps: three rectangles in a row labelled "1. Slice the bread", "2. Spread the butter", "3. Top with chives and salt", connected by arrows from 1 to 2 to 3
5. What goes where: arrows from each ingredient to the step that uses it (bread to 1, butter to 2, chives and salt to 3)
6. Colors: "butter" filled yellow, "chives" filled green, all other shapes keep no fill
7. Tip: a sticky note near step 3 with the text "Chop the chives just before serving"
8. Done: an ellipse "Enjoy!" after step 3 with an arrow from step 3, then zoomed to fit, all text readable, nothing selected
