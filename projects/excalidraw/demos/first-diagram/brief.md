# first-diagram

## Audience

People who have never used Excalidraw. They know what a diagram is, nothing about the tool.

## Goal

The viewer understands that Excalidraw opens straight to a blank canvas and that a small
diagram takes a few clicks: draw boxes, type labels, connect them with an arrow, add color.

## Story

A guest opens excalidraw.com without signing in. They draw a box labelled "Idea", a second box
labelled "Diagram", connect them with an arrow and fill the first box light blue. The video ends
on the finished diagram.

## Key moments

1. Empty canvas: the blank canvas with the toolbar at the top and the welcome screen
2. First box: a rectangle labelled "Idea" on the canvas
3. Second box: a second rectangle labelled "Diagram" to the right of the first
4. Arrow: an arrow points from "Idea" to "Diagram"
5. Color: "Idea" is selected and filled light blue, the Background colors are visible in the panel
6. Finished diagram: both boxes and the arrow, nothing selected

## Do not show

Opening or using Live collaboration, sharing, Sign up, the Excalidraw+ trial page, export to
the cloud or any login. The welcome screen lists some of these and the "Try Excalidraw+" link
and the share button stay visible in the corner, but they are never hovered or clicked.

## Format

About 40 seconds, 1280x720 browser (2560x1440 video), `first-diagram.mp4`. For a first look at the tool, no audio.
