# {{demo}}

What the person wants to show. The agent fills this with the person before writing scene.py and
records nothing until they confirm it.

## Audience

Who watches, what they already know about the app and the domain.

## Goal

The one thing the viewer should understand or believe after watching.

## Story

The situation the demo plays out: which role, which demo records, what happens first and last.

## Key moments

Each item becomes one chapter in scene.py with exactly the title before the colon. The runner
reports items without a chapter in result.json as `missing_moments`.

1. First moment: what must be visible on screen when this chapter ends
2. Second moment: ...

## Do not show

Screens, data or features to keep out of the video (unfinished parts, internal data).

## Format

Target length, resolution if not the project default, output file name, where it will be used.
